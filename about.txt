The AI Job Search Blueprint
The core idea

A practical AI-powered job-search system for non-technical professionals.

Instead of using AI just to generate another generic resume or cover letter, the Blueprint uses 50 structured prompts to take someone through the entire job-search process — from understanding their value and finding the right roles to applications, LinkedIn, networking, interviews, salary negotiation, and follow-up.

The book repeatedly emphasizes using AI without inventing qualifications, experience, achievements, or numbers.


The book is organized into 10 sections and 50 prompts, followed by two bonus resources. The table of contents runs through the 10 sections from pages 2–61, with the bonuses on pages 62–65.

01 — KNOW YOUR VALUE

Prompts 01–05

Before applying for jobs, the reader first figures out what they actually bring to the table.

Includes:

01 — Discover Your Transferable Skills
Identify skills from existing experience that can transfer into other roles.

02 — Find Your Professional Strengths
Discover strengths, valuable skills, problems you're good at solving, and environments where you may perform best.

03 — Turn Responsibilities Into Achievements
Transform boring responsibility-based resume bullets into achievement-focused statements.

04 — Find Your Professional Differentiator
Identify what genuinely separates the candidate from people with similar backgrounds.

05 — Create Your Professional Positioning Statement
Create different versions of a professional introduction for networking, interviews, LinkedIn and resumes.

The outcome:

"I know what I bring to the table."

02 — FIND THE RIGHT JOB

Prompts 06–10

This section prevents people from blindly applying to hundreds of jobs.

Includes:

06 — Discover Suitable Job Titles
Generate realistic target roles and separate them into:

Strong matches
Adjacent roles
Stretch roles

07 — Find Adjacent Career Paths
Discover realistic career transitions that build upon existing experience.

08 — Decode a Job Description
Break down complicated job descriptions into requirements, responsibilities, skills, terminology and what the employer is actually looking for.

09 — Score Your Fit for a Job
Evaluate how well the candidate matches a particular position.

10 — Identify Your Skill Gaps
Separate gaps into:

Critical
Important
Nice to have

and determine what to work on first.

The outcome:

"I know which jobs are actually worth pursuing."

03 — BUILD YOUR RESUME

Prompts 11–15

This is the resume-building section.

Includes:

11 — Audit Your Resume
Get a hiring-manager-style review covering clarity, relevance, impact, weak language, missing information and readability.

12 — Write a Strong Professional Summary
Generate different professional-summary versions.

13 — Rewrite Your Experience Section
Turn responsibilities into concise, action-oriented, achievement-focused bullets.

14 — Find the Numbers in Your Experience
Identify places where real metrics could strengthen the resume — revenue, customers, time saved, team size, projects, growth, efficiency, etc.

15 — Build a Targeted Resume
Adapt the existing resume specifically for a target job while keeping everything truthful.

The outcome:

"My resume actually communicates my value."

04 — OPTIMIZE FOR ATS & APPLICATIONS

Prompts 16–20

This section focuses on matching the application to what employers are actually asking for.

Includes:

16 — Extract the Important Keywords

Identify important:

Skills
Qualifications
Tools
Responsibilities
Industry terminology

17 — Find the Matches in Your Resume

Compare job requirements against actual evidence in the resume.

18 — Improve ATS Alignment

Improve terminology and alignment without keyword stuffing or falsely claiming skills.

19 — Find Why Your Application Could Be Rejected

Think like a hiring manager and identify realistic reasons the application might be overlooked.

20 — Final Application Check

A final quality-control pass before submitting.

The outcome:

"My application is relevant, targeted and ready to submit."

Prompts 31–35

Includes:

31 — Find the Right People to Contact

33 — Message a Recruiter

08 — PREPARE FOR THE INTERVIEW

Prompts 36–40

The Blueprint then moves into interview preparation.

Includes:

36 — Predict the Interview Questions

Generate likely questions based on the role and resume.

37 — Build Strong Answers

Structure answers around:

Situation → Action → Result

38 — Explain Your Career Change

Create an honest explanation of why you're moving between roles or industries.

39 — Handle Your Weaknesses

Prepare answers around skill gaps, lack of experience or employment gaps.

40 — Run a Mock Interview

AI acts as the hiring manager, asks questions one at a time, evaluates responses and gives improvement suggestions.

The outcome:

"I don't walk into the interview wondering what they'll ask."

Prompts 41–45

Includes:

41 — Research Your Salary Range

Estimate a reasonable salary range based on role, location, industry and experience.

42 — Answer "What Are Your Salary Expectations?"

Prepare different ways of answering the question.

43 — Evaluate a Job Offer

Look beyond salary:

Base salary
Bonus
Benefits
Working arrangement
Hours
Growth
Responsibilities
Stability
Other terms

44 — Negotiate Your Offer

Identify potential areas for negotiation.

45 — Decide Whether to Accept

Score the offer according to the candidate's own priorities and understand the trade-offs rather than blindly accepting or rejecting.

The outcome:

"Getting the offer isn't the end — I know how to evaluate it."

10 — FOLLOW-UP & BUILDING MOMENTUM

Prompts 46–50

The final section turns the prompts into an ongoing system.

Includes:

46 — Follow Up After an Interview

Create professional email and LinkedIn follow-ups.

47 — Follow Up on an Application

Politely ask about application status.

48 — Learn From a Rejection

Analyze what can realistically be learned from rejection without pretending to know why the candidate was rejected.

49 — Build Your Job Search Plan

Create a realistic weekly plan covering applications, networking, recruiters, interviews, follow-ups and tracking.

50 — Build Your Personal Job Search System

Combine the entire Blueprint into a repeatable job-search workflow.

The outcome:

"I now have a repeatable system instead of randomly applying."

🎁 BONUS 01 — THE 50-PROMPT ROADMAP

A quick roadmap showing the progression of the 50 prompts.

The idea is essentially:

Know yourself → Find your target → Build your resume → Optimize → Build your presence → Apply → Network → Interview → Evaluate offers → Build your system.

The roadmap specifically covers identifying job titles, researching career paths, evaluating job descriptions, assessing company culture, finding skill gaps, considering industry trends, using job boards/networks, informational interviews, career assessments and setting realistic career goals.
